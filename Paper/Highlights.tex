\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
    \item WOW Air bankruptcy created a major negative tourism supply shock in Iceland
    \item Monthly departing passengers fell by more than 20\% after bankruptcy
    \item Travel did not recover within the 9 months prior to Covid. 
    \item Resulting labor losses were large and concentrated in tourism-linked sectors
    \item Findings show labor vulnerability in tourism-dependent small economies
\end{itemize}
