\textbf{Author Biography} \\
Luke F. Miller is a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Hawai‘i at Mānoa. His research focuses on applied and empirical economics, using causal inference methods to study tourism, economic development, and the economic dimensions of energy and climate change. His work examines how shocks and policy interventions influence economic outcomes, with particular interest in poverty alleviation and economic resilience in low-income and vulnerable regions. He has professional experience in energy and applied policy research, collaborating with government agencies and nonprofit organizations on evidence-based program evaluation.
